About Education in the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom is one of the world's most prestigious study destinations, renowned for its academic excellence, shorter course durations, and globally respected qualifications. Home to some of the world's oldest and highest-ranked universities, the UK offers Nepali students access to high-quality education, strong career prospects, and valuable post-study work opportunities through the Graduate Route visa.

The UK education system emphasizes independent learning, critical thinking, research skills, and practical assessment methods. Bachelor's degrees are typically completed in three years, while most Master's programs take just one year — making the UK a cost-effective and time-efficient choice.

Why Choose the United Kingdom?

  • World-renowned universities with strong international reputations
  • Shorter course durations that help reduce overall study costs
  • Post-study work through the Graduate Route visa (2 years / 3 years PhD)
  • Wide range of scholarships and funding opportunities
  • High academic standards with globally recognized qualifications

Requirements

Key Requirements

Academic Qualification

Minimum GPA 2.8 for bachelor's programs. Minimum 50% in bachelor's degree for postgraduate. Study gaps accepted with valid justification.

  • SEE + Grade 12 with minimum GPA 2.8 for undergraduate entry
  • Bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks for master's programs
  • Study gap of 2–5 years accepted for master's with documented explanation
  • Notarized/attested transcripts and degree certificates required
  • NEB equivalency required for foreign board certificates

English Proficiency

IELTS (UKVI) 6.0 for bachelor's / IELTS (UKVI) 6.5 for master's. PTE Academic also accepted by most UK universities.

  • IELTS (Academic/UKVI): 6.0 for undergraduate, 6.5 for postgraduate
  • PTE Academic: 51 for bachelor's, 58 for master's
  • Some universities accept Duolingo English Test (85+)
  • English-medium schooling may be considered for conditional offers
  • Test scores must be valid within 2 years of application date

Financial Proof

Funds must be held in bank for at least 28 consecutive days before the visa application. Income sources include salary, property rent, and business.

  • Pre-sessional / foundation: approx. NPR 25–35 Lakhs
  • Bachelor's programs: approx. NPR 40–55 Lakhs
  • Master's programs: approx. NPR 35–50 Lakhs
  • 28-day bank balance rule strictly enforced by UK Home Office
  • Education loan sanction letter also accepted as financial evidence

UK Student Visa Documentation

CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) from a licensed UK sponsor institution is mandatory for the student visa application.

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months beyond course end date)
  • CAS number issued by a UKVI-licensed university or college
  • Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) fee payment confirmation
  • SOP, 2–3 LORs, academic transcripts, and financial documents
  • Tuberculosis (TB) test certificate from an approved clinic in Nepal

Career & Work

Work & Graduate Route

The UK offers strong work rights during and after studies, with a dedicated post-study work visa for graduates.

Part-Time Work During Studies

UK student visa holders are permitted to work up to 20 hours per week during term time. During official vacation periods, full-time work is allowed. Minimum wage in the UK is currently GBP £11.44/hour (age 21+).

Graduate Route Visa (Post-Study Work)

After completing a UK degree, graduates are eligible for the Graduate Route visa — 2 years for bachelor's and master's graduates, 3 years for PhD graduates. No job offer is required. Work in any occupation, any employer, unlimited hours.

Skilled Worker Visa Pathway

After the Graduate Route, students with a job offer from a UK employer can switch to a Skilled Worker visa, which can lead to Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) after 5 years — a pathway to permanent residency in the UK.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a bachelor's and master's degree take in the UK? +

Bachelor's degrees in the UK are typically 3 years (4 years in Scotland). Most master's programs are 1 year full-time, which is significantly shorter than the 2-year programs in many other countries. This makes the UK very cost-effective in terms of both time and total investment.

What is the 28-day bank balance rule for the UK student visa? +

UK Home Office requires that the required funds (tuition fee for first year + £1,334/month for living costs in London, or £1,023/month outside London) must be held continuously in your bank account for at least 28 consecutive days before you apply for the student visa. The funds must still be available on the date of application.

Can I work while studying in the UK on a student visa? +

Yes. UK student visa holders are permitted to work up to 20 hours per week during term time. During official vacation periods (Christmas, Easter, summer), you can work full-time. The current UK National Living Wage is GBP £11.44/hour (age 21+), making part-time work a meaningful income supplement.

What is the Graduate Route visa and how do I apply? +

The Graduate Route visa allows UK university graduates to remain in the UK and work for 2 years (3 years for PhD graduates) after completing their degree. There is no requirement for a job offer, and you can work in any sector. You must apply from within the UK before your student visa expires. Sukriti Global provides full support for this process.

What are the total costs of studying in the UK for Nepali students? +

Tuition fees for international students typically range from GBP £10,000–20,000/year for undergraduate programs and GBP £12,000–25,000 for a one-year master's. Living costs add approximately GBP £10,000–18,000/year. The 1-year master's is particularly cost-effective as total costs are much lower than equivalent 2-year programs in the USA or Australia.

When are UK university intakes and application deadlines? +

The primary intake is in September (autumn), for which applications typically open from October the previous year. Selected universities also offer a January intake with limited programs. For undergraduate programs, most applications go through UCAS. Postgraduate applications are made directly to the university. We recommend applying at least 6–9 months before your intended start date.
