About Education in New Zealand

Embark on your academic journey in New Zealand, a country known for its world-class institutions, advanced research opportunities, and excellent career pathways for Nepali students seeking global success. New Zealand's education system includes universities, Institutes of Technology and Polytechnics (ITPs), and Private Training Establishments (PTEs).

Most bachelor's programs are three years in duration, while master's programs generally take one to two years. New Zealand institutions focus strongly on practical learning, research excellence, innovation, and industry connections — helping graduates become highly employable in the international job market. Major academic intakes are in February and July.

Why Choose New Zealand?

  • Globally recognized education system
  • High graduate employability and work opportunities
  • Post-study work and PR pathways
  • Safe, peaceful, and student-friendly environment
  • Affordable education with a high quality of life

Requirements

Key Requirements

Academic Qualification

Minimum GPA 2.8 for undergraduate entry. Bachelor's degree with minimum 50% for postgraduate. Study gaps accepted with proper documentation.

  • SEE + Grade 12 with minimum GPA 2.8 for bachelor's programs
  • Bachelor's degree (minimum 50%) required for master's programs
  • Study gap of 2–5 years accepted with valid justification letter
  • Foundation or diploma programs available for direct university entry
  • Notarized/attested transcripts and degree certificates required

English Language Proficiency

IELTS 6.0 overall for bachelor's programs. IELTS 6.5 overall for master's programs. Equivalent PTE Academic scores also accepted.

  • IELTS Academic: minimum 6.0 for undergraduate, 6.5 for postgraduate
  • PTE Academic: equivalent score to IELTS requirement (typically 50/58)
  • TOEFL iBT accepted by select universities (79+ for bachelor's)
  • English-medium schooling documentation may be accepted
  • Test scores must be valid within 2 years of application

Financial Proof

Sufficient funds to cover tuition and living expenses. Income from salary, property rental, or business accepted as financial evidence.

  • Diploma / Foundation programs: approx. NPR 20–30 Lakhs
  • Bachelor's programs: approx. NPR 35–50 Lakhs
  • Master's programs: approx. NPR 30–45 Lakhs
  • Income sources: salary, house rent, land lease, business documents
  • Education loan sanction letter also accepted as proof of funds

Student Visa Documentation

Offer letter from a New Zealand institution, valid passport, financial evidence, and health/character declarations required for the student visa.

  • Valid passport (minimum 3 months beyond intended stay)
  • Offer letter from a New Zealand NZQA-registered institution
  • Proof of financial means (bank statements or loan letter)
  • Medical and chest X-ray certificates (TB test from approved clinic)
  • Police clearance certificate and character declaration

Career & Work

Work & Post-Study Opportunities

New Zealand provides strong work rights during studies and clear post-study pathways including PR for eligible graduates.

Part-Time Work During Studies

International students can work up to 25 hours per week during academic sessions, and full-time during scheduled breaks and holidays. Expanded work rights now also include approved exchange and Study Abroad programs. New Zealand minimum wage is NZD $23.15/hour.

Post-Study Work Visa

Graduates of bachelor's and postgraduate programs remain eligible for Post-Study Work Visas, allowing graduates to work in New Zealand for up to 3 years after completing their qualification. No specific job offer required to apply.

PR & Residence Pathways

New Zealand offers clear residency pathways for skilled graduates. The Skilled Migrant Category and Green List occupations (nurses, engineers, IT professionals, etc.) provide direct pathways to permanent residence for qualified graduates with NZ work experience.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours can I work while studying in New Zealand? +

International students enrolled in full-time degree programs at New Zealand universities and ITPs are permitted to work up to 25 hours per week during academic sessions. During official holidays and semester breaks, you can work full-time without restriction. New Zealand's minimum wage is NZD $23.15/hour, making part-time work a valuable income source.

What are the main intake dates for New Zealand institutions? +

New Zealand universities and institutions offer two main intakes — February (Semester 1) and July (Semester 2). Most programs are available in both intakes, though some competitive programs may only have a February start. Private Training Establishments (PTEs) may offer rolling monthly intakes. We recommend applying at least 4–6 months before your intended intake.

Can I get PR after studying in New Zealand? +

Yes. New Zealand offers clear PR pathways for skilled graduates. After completing your degree and gaining work experience, you can apply for the Skilled Migrant Category visa. New Zealand's Green List includes high-demand occupations (nurses, engineers, IT professionals, construction workers) that offer direct residence or fast-track pathways for eligible applicants.

How long does a New Zealand student visa take to process? +

New Zealand student visa processing typically takes 4–8 weeks when all required documents are submitted correctly through the Immigration New Zealand online portal. Medical certificates and police clearance should be arranged in advance as they can take additional time. Sukriti Global provides complete documentation support to minimize delays.

What is the Post-Study Work Visa in New Zealand? +

The Post-Study Work Visa allows graduates from bachelor's, postgraduate diploma, or master's programs to work in New Zealand for up to 3 years after completing their qualification. The duration depends on the study level and the region of study. No specific job offer is needed to apply. It can be applied for from inside New Zealand before your student visa expires.

What is the total cost of studying in New Zealand for Nepali students? +

Tuition fees for international students range from NZD $20,000–35,000/year for undergraduate programs and NZD $25,000–45,000/year for postgraduate programs. Diploma and foundation programs cost approximately NZD $15,000–25,000/year. Living costs typically add NZD $15,000–24,000/year. Scholarships and part-time work income can significantly reduce the overall financial burden.
