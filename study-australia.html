Study Abroad

About Education in Australia

Australia is one of the most preferred study destinations for Nepali students because of its globally recognized education system, multicultural society, and strong post-study work opportunities. Australian institutions focus on practical learning, industry exposure, and career development, helping students become highly competitive in the global job market.

With flexible academic pathways, transparent visa procedures, and strong student protection policies, Australia remains an excellent destination for undergraduate, postgraduate, and vocational studies.

  • Globally ranked universities and colleges
  • Strong post-study work and migration opportunities
  • High acceptance of Nepali academic qualifications
  • Safe, multicultural, and student-friendly environment
  • Part-time work opportunities during study

Requirements

Education System & Requirements

Academic Qualification

Minimum GPA of 2.8 or above in SEE and +2 (Grade 12) or equivalent qualification.

  • SEE + Grade 12 with minimum GPA 2.8 or equivalent marks
  • Certified and attested academic transcripts from all institutions
  • NEB equivalency letter if using a foreign board certificate
  • Study gap of 2–5 years may be accepted for master's applicants with proper justification
  • Clear explanation and supporting documents for any gap years

English Language Requirements

IELTS or PTE scores required based on program level. Health programs require a valid CEE certificate.

  • Bachelor's programs: IELTS 6.0 overall or equivalent PTE score
  • Master's programs: IELTS 6.5 overall or equivalent PTE score
  • Health & medical programs (Nursing, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Dental, Medicine): valid CEE certificate is mandatory
  • Language test scores must be valid (within 2 years)

Financial Requirements

Verifiable income sources required. Estimated annual income shown below per program type.

  • Accepted income sources: salary, house rent, land lease, registered business
  • VET programs: approx. NPR 20 lakhs annual income
  • Bachelor's programs: approx. NPR 25–30 lakhs annual income
  • Master's programs: approx. NPR 25–29 lakhs annual income
  • Sponsor affidavit required if funded by family (with relationship proof)

Education Loans & Documentation

Education loans accepted from recognized Nepali A-Class banks. Typical loan limits apply per program.

  • VET courses: up to NPR 45 lakhs
  • Bachelor's programs: NPR 55–65 lakhs
  • Master's programs: up to NPR 55 lakhs
  • Valid passport (min. 12 months validity), SOP, academic transcripts, police clearance
  • Passport-size photos, Nepal citizenship/ID proof, and medical reports as required
Study Programs

Top Fields of Study in Australia

Australia offers world-class programs across diverse disciplines. Here are the most popular study fields chosen by Nepali students.

Nursing & Healthcare

One of the most in-demand fields for Nepali students. Australia has a strong demand for qualified nurses and healthcare workers with clear pathways to PR.

Requires: IELTS 7.0+ & valid CEE certificate

IT & Computer Science

High-demand field with excellent employment outcomes. Programs in software engineering, cybersecurity, AI, and data science are widely available.

Entry: IELTS 6.0–6.5 | Bachelor's or Master's

Business & Management

MBA and business degrees from Australian universities are globally respected, offering strong career outcomes in finance, marketing, and international business.

Entry: IELTS 6.0–6.5 | VET, Bachelor's or Master's

Engineering

Civil, mechanical, electrical, and mining engineering are among Australia's most sought-after programs with strong industry connections and job placements.

Entry: IELTS 6.0–6.5 | Bachelor's or Master's

Pharmacy & Allied Health

Pharmacy, physiotherapy, and dental surgery programs are popular among Nepali students. A valid CEE certificate is mandatory for all health-related programs in Australia.

Requires: IELTS 7.0+ & CEE certificate

Hospitality & Cookery (VET)

VET (Vocational Education and Training) programs in cookery, hospitality, and early childhood are popular entry pathways that often lead to PR through skilled migration.

Entry: IELTS 6.0 | Duration: 1–2 years

Career & Migration

Work & Career Opportunities in Australia

Australia offers one of the world's most generous post-study work rights and clear pathways to permanent residency for skilled graduates.

Post-Study Work Rights (Subclass 485)

After graduating, students can apply for the Temporary Graduate visa (Subclass 485) to work in Australia for 2–6 years depending on the qualification level and study location.

  • ✓ Bachelor's degree: 2 years post-study work visa
  • ✓ Master's degree: 3 years post-study work visa
  • ✓ Study in regional areas can extend by 1–2 extra years
  • ✓ Work unrestricted — any occupation, anywhere in Australia

Part-Time Work During Study

Australian student visa (Subclass 500) allows students to work while studying, helping cover living costs and gain local work experience.

  • ✓ Up to 48 hours per fortnight during term
  • ✓ Unlimited hours during scheduled course breaks
  • ✓ Minimum wage: AUD $23.23/hour (approx.)
  • ✓ Common roles: hospitality, retail, aged care, delivery

Permanent Residency (PR) Pathways

Australia's skilled migration program offers clear PR pathways for international graduates in high-demand occupations through skilled points-based visas.

  • ✓ Skilled Independent visa (Subclass 189)
  • ✓ Skilled Nominated visa (Subclass 190)
  • ✓ Regional Sponsored Migration (Subclass 491)
  • ✓ Nursing, IT, and engineering top the skilled occupation list

Student Life & Cost of Living

Australia is safe, diverse, and student-friendly. Monthly living costs vary by city, with Melbourne and Sydney being more expensive than regional areas.

  • AccommodationAUD $800–$1,500/month

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions Nepali students ask about studying in Australia.

What GPA do I need to apply for Australia? +

A minimum GPA of 2.8 or above in SEE and Grade 12 (or equivalent) is generally required. Some programs or institutions may have higher GPA requirements. Our counsellors will assess your academic background and match you with universities where you have the best chance of admission.

What is a CEE certificate and who needs it? +

The CEE (Common Entrance Examination) certificate is a mandatory qualification for applicants applying to health and medical-related programs in Australia — including Nursing, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Dental Surgery, and Medicine. It verifies that your academic background meets Australian health program standards.

Can I get an education loan for studying in Australia? +

Yes. Education loans from recognized Nepali A-Class banks are accepted. Typical loan limits are: up to NPR 45 lakhs for VET programs, NPR 55–65 lakhs for bachelor's programs, and up to NPR 55 lakhs for master's programs. Sukriti Global can guide you through the loan documentation process.

How much can I work while studying in Australia? +

Students on a Subclass 500 student visa can work up to 48 hours per fortnight during their course and unlimited hours during scheduled course breaks. Australia's minimum wage is approximately AUD $23.23 per hour, making part-time work a meaningful supplement to living costs.

Can I stay in Australia after completing my studies? +

Yes. Australia's Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485) allows graduates to work for 2–6 years after completing their degree. Depending on your occupation and state, you may also be eligible to apply for permanent residency through the Skilled Independent (189), Skilled Nominated (190), or Regional (491) visa pathways.

What is the Australia student visa and how long does it take? +

The Australia student visa (Subclass 500) allows you to study full-time in Australia. Processing times are typically 4–8 weeks but can vary. Sukriti Global prepares your complete visa file — including the Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), financial documents, GTE statement, and health insurance — to maximize your chances of approval.
