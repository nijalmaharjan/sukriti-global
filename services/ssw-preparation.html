About the SSW Program

The Specified Skilled Worker (SSW / 特定技能) visa is a Japanese work visa introduced in 2019 to address labor shortages in specific industries. It allows foreign nationals — including Nepali candidates — to legally live and work in Japan in designated industrial sectors.

At Sukriti Global Education Consultancy, we provide complete SSW pathway support: from Japanese language training and skill test preparation to visa documentation, job placement, and post-arrival assistance. Our expert team guides you every step of the way toward a successful career in Japan.

Visa Types

SSW Type (i) vs Type (ii)

Understand the key differences between SSW Type (i) and Type (ii) to choose the right pathway for your career goals in Japan.

SSW Type (i)

特定技能 1号
Language
JLPT N4 or JFT-Basic (A2 level)
Skill Test
Industry-specific Skill Evaluation Test required
Stay Period
Up to 5 years total (renewable annually)
Family
Family members cannot accompany
Industries
16 designated industry fields
Path to Type (ii): Eligible workers can transition to SSW Type (ii) in qualifying fields after demonstrating higher skill levels.

SSW Type (ii)

特定技能 2号
Language
JLPT N3 or higher (B1 level) recommended
Skill Test
Advanced skill test + supervisory skills
Stay Period
Unlimited renewals (no total cap)
Family
Spouse & children can accompany
Industries
11 designated industry fields
Path to PR: SSW Type (ii) holders are eligible to apply for Permanent Residency (PR) after 10 years of continuous stay in Japan.

Required Tests

SSW Examination Information

To qualify for SSW Type (i), candidates must pass both a Japanese language test and an industry-specific skill evaluation test.

JFT-Basic

Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese — measures A2 level Japanese language ability required for SSW Type (i) visa.

Level Required: A2 (Conversational Basic)
Format: Online test, multiple choice
Sections: Listening + Reading
Official JFT-Basic Website

JLPT

Japanese Language Proficiency Test — the most widely recognized Japanese certification. N4 or above accepted for SSW Type (i).

Level Required: N4 (SSW i) / N3+ (SSW ii)
Format: Paper-based, twice a year
Sections: Language Knowledge + Reading + Listening
Official JLPT Website
