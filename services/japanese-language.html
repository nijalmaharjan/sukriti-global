Curriculum

What You Will Learn

Our Japanese language program covers all four core language skills alongside the essential Japanese writing systems.

Speaking & Conversation

Learn to hold real conversations in Japanese. From introductions and daily expressions to workplace and interview dialogues, our speaking practice sessions build confidence and fluency from the very first class.

Listening Comprehension

Train your ear to understand natural Japanese speech. Students practice with audio exercises, mock JLPT listening tests, and real-world dialogues to develop the ability to comprehend spoken Japanese at various speeds and accents.

Reading

Progress from reading simple hiragana sentences to complex passages involving kanji and mixed scripts. Students learn to read notices, advertisements, short articles, and JLPT reading comprehension texts with accuracy and speed.

Writing & Grammar

Master the three Japanese writing scripts — Hiragana, Katakana, and Kanji — along with essential grammar patterns. Students learn to write sentences, compose messages, and express ideas in written Japanese with clarity and correctness.

Writing Systems

The Japanese Writing System

Japanese uses three distinct scripts. Our program teaches all three systematically so students can read and write with confidence.

あ

Hiragana

46 Characters

The foundational phonetic script of Japanese. Every beginner starts here. Hiragana is used for native Japanese words, grammar particles, verb endings, and to show pronunciation.

ア

Katakana

46 Characters

Used primarily for foreign loanwords, brand names, scientific terms, and emphasis. Recognizing katakana is essential in modern Japan as it appears widely in daily life, menus, and signage.

漢

Kanji

2,136 Common Characters

Chinese-origin characters used for nouns, verb stems, and adjectives. Students learn kanji progressively — from ~100 at N5 level up to 1,000+ at N2. Kanji mastery is key to reading and writing fluently.

Opportunities

Why Learn Japanese?

Japanese language skills open doors to education, employment, and life in one of the world's most advanced and culturally rich nations.

Study in Japan

Japanese language proficiency (N4 or higher) is required for admission to most Japanese language schools and universities. Start your academic journey in Japan with the right language foundation.

Work in Japan

Many Japanese companies require N3 or higher for employment. From manufacturing and hospitality to IT and healthcare, Japanese language skills are a key qualification for working in Japan across all industries.

SSW Visa Pathway

The Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) visa is Nepal's most popular route to Japan. Passing the JFT-Basic test or JLPT N4 is a mandatory requirement. Our Japanese language course directly prepares you for this pathway.

Cultural Understanding

Beyond language, our program includes Japanese cultural education — workplace etiquette, customs, social norms, and daily life in Japan — so students are fully prepared to adapt and thrive in a Japanese environment.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Japanese language learning at Sukriti Global.

Do I need any prior knowledge to join the Japanese language course? +

No prior knowledge is required. Our beginner course starts from absolute zero — you will learn hiragana, katakana, and basic vocabulary from the very first class. Students with some existing knowledge are placed in the appropriate level after a brief assessment.

How long does it take to become conversational in Japanese? +

With consistent daily practice and regular class attendance, most students can hold basic conversations within 3–4 months (N5 level). Achieving comfortable everyday communication typically takes 8–12 months of structured study through the N4 level.

Is Japanese language difficult to learn for Nepali students? +

Japanese has a different grammar structure and writing system compared to Nepali or English, but with the right guidance it is very manageable. At Sukriti Global, our instructors use Nepali-medium explanations where needed, making learning far more accessible and effective for Nepali students.

What is the difference between JLPT and JFT-Basic? +

The JLPT (Japanese Language Proficiency Test) has 5 levels (N5–N1) and is a globally recognized certification for academic and professional use. The JFT-Basic is a simpler test used specifically for the SSW visa pathway, testing only conversational Japanese needed for daily life and work in Japan. Both are covered in our training programs.

What class schedules are available? +

We offer morning, afternoon, and evening batches to suit students with different routines — whether you are a school graduate, a working professional, or preparing full-time for Japan. Contact us to find out current batch timings and availability.

Will I receive a certificate after completing the course? +

Yes. Upon successful completion of each level, students receive a course completion certificate from Sukriti Global Education Consultancy. Additionally, we guide students through the official JLPT and JFT-Basic registration so they can earn internationally recognized certifications.
