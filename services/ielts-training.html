Guide for Nepali Students

IELTS vs PTE: Which English Test is Better for You?

A comprehensive comparison to help Nepali students choose between IELTS and PTE Academic for study abroad applications.

Important: Both IELTS and PTE are accepted by universities in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, and Canada. The USA accepts both but has specific preferences by institution.
Understanding the Two Tests

IELTS vs PTE — Test Overview

IELTS

International English Language Testing System
The Traditional Choice — gold standard for English testing for decades

IELTS tests your English through four sections: Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking. Its defining feature is a face-to-face speaking interview with a certified examiner — allowing natural conversation but can feel intimidating.

For Nepali students, IELTS feels familiar — essay-style writing, traditional comprehension patterns, and a structured interview format similar to academic environments you've already experienced.

Format Paper-based or Computer-based
Scoring Band 0–9 (half-band increments)
Results 3–5 days (CBT) / 13 days (PBT)
Cost Nepal ~NPR 26,500
Speaking Human examiner (11–14 min)

PTE Academic

Pearson Test of English Academic
The Modern Alternative — fully computer-based, AI-scored, fast results

PTE is a fully computer-based test using artificial intelligence to score your English skills. Skills are tested in an integrated way — e.g., listen to a lecture and then summarize it in writing.

The speaking section is recorded on a computer — no human interviewer. This can be less intimidating, but requires comfort with speaking into a microphone while managing strict time limits.

Format Fully computer-based only
Scoring 10–90 points (AI-scored)
Results Within 48 hours
Cost Nepal ~NPR 25,000
Speaking AI-scored microphone recording

Which Test to Choose

Choose the Test That Fits You

The right choice depends on your individual strengths, timeline, and study abroad destination.

Choose IELTS if...

✓ You're comfortable with traditional pen-and-paper tests and face-to-face interviews
✓ Your writing skills are strong, especially for essay-style answers
✓ You prefer a human examiner who can adapt to your speaking pace
✓ You're applying to universities in the UK or institutions that explicitly prefer IELTS
✓ You have 2–3 months to prepare without a tight deadline
Enroll in IELTS Training

Choose PTE if...

✓ You're comfortable with computers and typing quickly and accurately
✓ You get nervous in face-to-face speaking interviews with a human examiner
✓ You need fast results for tight application deadlines (results in 48 hours)
✓ You're aiming for Australia or New Zealand where PTE is widely preferred
✓ You need a precise score (e.g., exactly 65 for Australian immigration)

Preparation Strategies

How to Prepare — Nepali Student Tips

Proven strategies tailored for Nepali students preparing for either test.

For IELTS Preparation

1. Develop essay writing skills and expand academic vocabulary through daily practice.
2. Practice speaking English regularly with friends or tutors to build confidence for the interview.
3. Listen to BBC, VOA, or Australian radio to familiarize yourself with different English accents.
4. Work on handwriting legibility if taking the paper-based version.
5. Take at least 3–4 full mock tests under timed, exam-condition settings.

For PTE Preparation

1. Practice typing quickly and accurately — you'll need to type all writing responses.
2. Get comfortable speaking clearly into a microphone without long pauses or hesitation.
3. Use the official PTE practice software to master the format and strict section timings.
4. Focus on integrated skills tasks: listen-then-summarize, read-then-speak, etc.
5. Practice with exposure to multiple accents — Australian, British, American, Canadian.

Common Challenges for Nepali Students

• Writing: Structuring academic essays and using formal vocabulary. Both tests require practice here.
• IELTS Speaking: Nervousness in face-to-face interview situations is common for Nepali students.
• PTE Speaking: Managing strict time limits while speaking clearly into a microphone.
• Listening: Exposure to multiple English accents — especially Australian — is important for both tests.

Final Advice

Making Your Decision

After working with countless Nepali students on this decision, our straightforward advice is: take a practice test for both. Many coaching centers in Nepal offer diagnostic tests for both IELTS and PTE. Your performance on these practice tests will give you the clearest indication of which format suits you better.

Consider your timeline too. If you have months to prepare and prefer traditional testing, IELTS might be your choice. If you need results quickly for an upcoming application deadline, PTE's 48-hour turnaround could be decisive.

Remember: both tests are valid measures of English proficiency. The "better" test is the one where you can achieve the score you need. Many students successfully take both tests and submit the higher score.
