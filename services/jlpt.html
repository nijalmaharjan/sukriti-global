JLPT Levels

Levels We Offer

We prepare students from beginner to intermediate level — N5 through N3 — with structured, level-specific curricula.

N5
Beginner Level

The entry-level JLPT certification. Suitable for students who have just started learning Japanese.

  • ✓ Basic hiragana, katakana & kanji (~100 characters)
  • ✓ ~800 vocabulary words
  • ✓ Simple sentences and everyday expressions
  • ✓ Basic listening & reading comprehension
  • ✓ Ideal for Japan travel & daily communication
N4
Elementary Level

For students who can understand basic Japanese used in everyday situations and familiar topics.

  • ✓ ~300 kanji & ~1,500 vocabulary words
  • ✓ Grammar for common everyday situations
  • ✓ Short conversations & passages
  • ✓ Listening from slow, clear Japanese speech
  • ✓ Required for many Japanese language school admissions
N3
Intermediate Level

A critical milestone. N3 bridges the gap between basic and advanced Japanese and is widely recognized by employers.

  • ✓ ~650 kanji & ~3,750 vocabulary words
  • ✓ Reading newspapers, notices & social content
  • ✓ Natural-speed conversations & narratives
  • ✓ Required for SSW visa & most Japan jobs
  • ✓ Accepted by many Japanese universities

Exam Information

JLPT Exam Details

Everything you need to know about the official Japanese Language Proficiency Test.

Exam Dates

The JLPT is held twice a year worldwide:

  • July First Sunday of July
  • December First Sunday of December

Results are typically released 1–2 months after the exam date.

Registration

Registration is done online through the official JLPT website. Key points:

  • ✓ Registration opens ~3 months before exam
  • ✓ Register at jlpt.jp or through authorized centers
  • ✓ Sukriti Global assists with the full registration process
  • ✓ Admit card issued before the exam date

Scoring & Passing

The JLPT uses a scaled scoring system:

  • ✓ Total score: 180 points (N5/N4) or 180 points (N3)
  • ✓ Pass mark: 90/180 for N5–N3
  • ✓ Each section has a minimum required score
  • ✓ Certificate valid for lifetime — no expiry
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about JLPT preparation at Sukriti Global.

Which JLPT level should I start with? +

If you have no prior Japanese knowledge, we recommend starting with N5. Students who have some basic Japanese background may begin with N4. Our counsellors will assess your current level and guide you to the right starting point during your free counselling session.

How long does it take to prepare for each level? +

Preparation time varies by level: N5 typically takes 3–4 months, N4 takes 4–6 months, and N3 takes 6–8 months for students with prior N4 knowledge. Our flexible class schedules ensure you can prepare at a pace that suits your routine.

Is JLPT required for working in Japan? +

Yes, for many Japan work pathways. The Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) visa typically requires N4 or passing the JFT-Basic test. Many Japanese companies and language schools require N3 or higher. N3 is also recognized as a key language requirement for employment in various industries in Japan.

Does Sukriti Global help with JLPT exam registration? +

Yes. We guide students through the entire JLPT registration process — from creating an account on the official website to submitting the application. We also notify students about registration deadlines and exam schedules well in advance.

What study materials are provided? +

Students receive comprehensive study materials including level-specific textbooks, grammar notes, vocabulary lists, and printed mock tests. Instructors also share supplementary resources and practice sheets throughout the course to reinforce learning.

Can I join mid-course if I have some Japanese knowledge? +

Yes. We conduct a brief assessment to evaluate your current Japanese proficiency and place you in the appropriate batch and level. You do not need to start from the very beginning if you already have foundational knowledge.
