Who We Are

Nepal's Most Trusted Education Partner

Sukriti Global Education Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. is a professional education consultancy and Japanese language training institution dedicated to helping students and professionals achieve their academic and career goals abroad.

With a strong focus on Japan — including Japanese language education, student visa support, and the Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) program — we provide reliable guidance, quality training, and personalized support for individuals seeking international opportunities.

Our institution is supported by experienced and professional team members committed to honest counseling and student-focused services, working in collaboration with partner organizations in Japan to help students and skilled workers access genuine educational and career opportunities.

Through professional guidance, strong international networks, and dedicated support, we prepare students for successful futures in Japan and beyond.

  • Japanese language education & JLPT/JFT preparation
  • Student visa counseling & documentation assistance
  • Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) program support
  • Interview training & cultural orientation
  • Partner networks & career support in Japan

Our Global Reach

Beyond Japan, Worldwide Guidance

Beyond Japan, the consultancy also provides comprehensive guidance for students planning to pursue higher education in leading destinations such as Australia, United States, New Zealand, and various European countries including United Kingdom, Germany, and Denmark. From university selection and admission assistance to visa counseling and pre-departure preparation, the organization is committed to guiding students at every step of their international journey.

With a strong focus on academic excellence, cultural understanding, and career development, Sukriti Global Education Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. aims to bridge Nepal with world-class educational opportunities. The consultancy believes in transforming ambitions into achievements by equipping students with the knowledge, confidence, and global perspective needed to succeed internationally and build a brighter future.
