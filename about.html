Our Story

Nepal's Premier Education Consultancy

Sukriti Global Education Consultancy Pvt. Ltd., is a professionally managed and purpose-driven educational consultancy committed to delivering high-quality Japanese language education, cultural training, and career guidance for students and professionals aspiring to study and work in Japan.

Located in Kathmandu, Nepal, our institution provides comprehensive Japanese language programs, cultural orientation, and professional support services designed to prepare individuals for successful academic and professional futures in Japan. We are dedicated to developing not only language proficiency but also the practical communication skills, discipline, confidence, and cultural understanding necessary to thrive in Japanese educational institutions and professional environments.

With a strong focus on quality education and personalized student support, we offer JLPT and JFT preparation classes, student visa counseling, Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) preparation, interview training, and complete documentation assistance. Our experienced instructors and counseling team work closely with each student to provide proper guidance and continuous support throughout every stage of their journey.

At Sukriti Global Education Consultancy Pvt. Ltd., we believe in professionalism, transparency, integrity, and long-term student success. Our mission is to build a strong bridge between Nepal and Japan by empowering individuals with the knowledge, skills, and confidence required to achieve their educational and career goals internationally.
